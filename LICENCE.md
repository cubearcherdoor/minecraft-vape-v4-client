## ❓ FAQ

**What is a ghost client?**
A ghost client is a cheat client designed for closet cheating — its modules mimic legitimate play, making them harder to detect than blatant clients like Wurst.

**Is this the real Vape?**
No — this is an open-source project inspired by Vape.gg. The official Vape is a paid, closed-source client.
